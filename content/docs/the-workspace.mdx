---
title: The Builder Workspace
description: The four regions of the UI Builder and what each one is for.
---

The UI Builder is where you actually assemble the dashboard. It's split into
four regions, and everything you do lives in one of them.

<Screenshot
  src="SS-04.jpg"
  alt="The UI Builder workspace with its top bar, Layers Panel, Canvas and Properties Panel"
  caption="The four regions of the UI Builder."
/>

- **Top bar**: shows the breadcrumb (Home / Test Dashboard) on the left, and
  your **Preview**, **Save**, and **Publish** buttons on the right. These three
  are how you check, keep, and ship your work (covered later).
- **Layers Panel** (far left), where you build the structure. It has a **Tabs**
  view and a **Components** view. Once a tab is selected, it lists that tab's
  **Sections**, **Line Items**, **Documents**, **Actions**, and **Components**,
  each with a `+` to add one. Think of this as the outline of your dashboard.
- **Canvas** (center), a live preview that updates as you build. You can switch
  between desktop and mobile view and zoom/pan around it. (Mobile view isn't
  fully built out yet, stay on desktop.)
- **Properties Panel** (far right), shows the settings for whatever you've
  clicked on (a section, a field, etc.). It stays empty until you select
  something, then fills with that item's options.

<Callout>
When you first open it, nothing is selected, so most panels say "Select a
tab...". That's expected. The first real step is to create a tab.
</Callout>
